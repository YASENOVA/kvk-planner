KvK Planner

Prep week appointments and castle battle planning

Introduction

One event covers the whole KvK. Prep week collects resource levels and schedules the King's Buff appointments across construction, research and training. Battle day collects who is online each hour and what troops they can field, then helps you split the server into teams and hand out Sanctuary and Fortress rewards.

Players get one link for both. You get a planning view for each, a public prep schedule and a public battle line-up you open when the teams are final.
